\section{The Tension Triplet}

Three independent probes give:
\begin{align}
H_0^{\text{CHIME}} &= 74.4 \pm 1.0 \text{ km/s/Mpc} \\
H_0^{\text{Pantheon+}} &= 73.4 \pm 1.0 \text{ km/s/Mpc} \\
w_{\text{DESI}} &= -0.65 \pm 0.05
\end{align}

The $\sim$2\% $H_0$ difference is within errors. The critical input is $w < -1/3$.

\section{Geometric Closure}

For $w < -1/3$, the Friedmann equation requires positive curvature. The curvature radius is:

\begin{equation}
R = \frac{c}{H_0}\sqrt{\frac{-3w}{1+w}} = 60.3 \text{ Gpc}
\end{equation}

using $H_0 = 73.9$ km/s/Mpc average. The circumference follows:

\begin{equation}
C = 2\pi R = 379 \text{ Gpc}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
f = \frac{c}{C} = \frac{299792458 \text{ m/s}}{379 \text{ Gpc}} = 472,789,997.636 \text{ Hz}
